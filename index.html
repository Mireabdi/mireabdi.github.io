about

My interest in technology didn't start in school. I've been helping family and friends with computer problems since I was a kid, installing Windows and chasing down hardware faults. It's always just felt like my thing.

At Haaga-Helia I've studied networks, operating systems, and security. Alongside my studies I've built a hands-on portfolio on GitHub, all of it backed up with screenshots and real troubleshooting cases.

Five years in customer service taught me what IT support takes: listen first, solve second. A frustrated user doesn't need a technical lecture. They need a fix.

Right now I'm looking for a first role in helpdesk or service desk work. Longer term, I want this to grow into a long career in IT, not just a first job.
